The SciGRID_gas IGGIELGN data set (v1.1.2) is third-party licensed and is NOT
redistributed in this package.

Download it from Zenodo:

    https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4767098

and unpack it so that the CSV files sit in this directory:

    scigrid/data/IGGIELGN_PipeSegments.csv
    scigrid/data/IGGIELGN_Nodes.csv
    ...

`work/extract_subnet.py` then reproduces `work/scigrid_subnet.json`
bit-identically (verify with md5sum over repeated runs).
